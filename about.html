Our Story

How It All Started

The Dubai Youth Economics Review was founded in 2021 by a small group of students who believed economics should not only exist in textbooks. They created a digital publication where students apply economic ideas to real events, covering developments in the UAE, global markets, and international politics. What began as a small online newsletter grew into a full publication with a team of writers, editors, and designers who research, write, and edit every article themselves.

The publication analyses real world events through an economic lens. Articles apply ideas from economic theory to current issues, using concepts such as loss aversion from behavioural economics and Nash equilibrium from game theory to explain developments in markets, policy, and international relations. The Dubai Youth Economics Review remains entirely student-led and online, committed to connecting classroom economics with the real world.
